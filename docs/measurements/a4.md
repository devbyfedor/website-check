# A4: mandatory information and system detection, local measurement

Session A4, 22.11.2026. Local Playwright adapter, Chromium 159, from Germany, the 30 test sites of `local/sites.txt` (homepage plus the Impressum in a second tab, no interaction), 15:01 to 15:07. Aggregated numbers and anonymous labels only; per-site data stays in `local/`.

## What the scan does

- On the homepage the snapshot marks every link that sits in the footer. The scanner takes the Impressum, Datenschutz and Erklärung zur Barrierefreiheit links by their German and English texts, footer first, and only when no text matches by the link address. It never guesses an address such as /impressum.
- The Impressum link is opened in a second tab of the same browser context after the homepage is done (no new browser, the same unconsented visit). The tab waits for DOMContentLoaded and then only until the text keeps its length (0.5 s after the load event, 1.5 s before it or while the text is shorter than 300 characters; at most 4 s after DOMContentLoaded, 16 s for the whole tab). It reads title, text and links and closes. No axe on the Impressum (Fedor, 22.11.2026), no request analysis, no screenshot in production (the local run saves one for the manual check, outside the measured time).
- Templates find the postal address, the e-mail (plain, mailto, "[at]", "(at)", "{at}", "[ät]", " at " with an e-mail label, "[dot]", "(dot)", " dot ", "[punkt]", Cloudflare's e-mail protection), the Handelsregister (HRA or HRB with number and court) and the USt-IdNr (DE plus 9 digits). Every field is found with its exact quote or "nicht gefunden, bitte manuell prüfen". Data that the text marks as someone else's (agency, hosting, photographer, arbitration board, chamber, authority, court, insurer, external data protection officer, the venue of a course) is skipped.
- System detection from headers, request URLs, the final URL, generator tags, globals and selectors; signals and sources in `docs/cms-fingerprints.md`.

## Browser time of the Impressum

The second tab was opened on 30 of 30 sites (every homepage has an Impressum link that leads to an HTML page on the same site).

- Browser time per Impressum tab, from opening the tab to closing it: median 1.2 s, mean 1.3 s, minimum 0.8 s, maximum 4.3 s; 1 of 30 tabs over 2 s. All 30 stopped because the text was stable, none at the 4 s limit.
- DOMContentLoaded after opening the tab: median 0.4 s, maximum 3.0 s. Requests of the tab (counted, not analysed): median 54, maximum 334.
- For the whole run: 40.2 s of Impressum tabs for 30 sites.

Capacity estimate for the free plan (10 browser minutes per day), with the A3 numbers for the homepage on Cloudflare and the local Impressum time instead of A3's assumption that the Impressum takes as long as the homepage: mean 4.7 s homepage + 1.05 s axe + 1.3 s Impressum + 0.6 s session = 7.7 s per full check, about 78 checks per day (A3 estimate: 54). Worst case unchanged in kind: a homepage at its 20 s limit plus an Impressum at its 16 s limit, 36.6 s, about 16 checks. The Impressum time on Cloudflare is not measured yet (A5).

## Manual check

Method:
- Ten sites were picked by a fixed rule before the run: every third site from Site 02 (Site 02, 05, 08, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26, 29: Shopify, WooCommerce 2, Shopware, WordPress 3, other, Wix, Jimdo).
- For each site an independent reviewer (a helper agent) got only the saved data: the homepage HTML and screenshot, the Impressum HTML, full-page screenshot and visible text, the main document headers and the request URLs. Not the scanner's result, and not which link the scanner had opened. No site was visited again.
- The reviewer extracted the three links, the four fields (with exact quotes, and how each is shown: text, image, absent) and the system by the same rules the scanner follows.
- Code compared the reviewer's values with the scanner's. Every difference went to a separate judge, who saw the same files and the two values as "A" and "B" without being told which came from the scanner.

First pass (the scanner as it was at the run): of 30 Impressum fields the scanner reported as found, 1 was wrong: on Site 26 the address of the local court, which the page lists under the heading "Aufsichtsbehörde", was taken for the operator's address (the operator's own address was missed because a site builder puts empty paragraphs between street and postcode). 4 fields present on the page as text were missed (two addresses, two Handelsregister entries). My own look at the found addresses of all 30 sites found a second wrong address outside the ten: the venue of a course in a sidebar ("Wo: …") on a page that had no operator address.

Fixes (general rules, each with a test on invented text, no rule for a single site): a street that starts with an umlaut after a short day name ("Fr", "Mo"), streets above empty paragraphs, house numbers up to three digits (a year is not a house number), and for addresses the two non-empty lines above the block are checked for a third-party label or a court line, across empty paragraphs. Re-analysed from the saved captures, no new visit.

Final (the scanner of the merge), Site 02 to 29:
- Impressum fields reported as found: 31, of them 31 right (30 the same as the reviewer, 1 the same address as the page writes it in its sidebar, which comes first in reading order, while the reviewer took the form of the body, which differs only by a space between the abbreviated street name and the house number; the judge took both as right). Wrong: 0.
- Links reported as found: 20 (10 Impressum, 10 Datenschutz), wrong: 0. Three differ from the reviewer's choice but lead to the same page: on Site 14 the scanner took the Impressum and Datenschutz links of the cookie dialog (its box has "footer" in its class name) because the footer's own Impressum link has no visible text; both lead to the same pages, with or without a trailing slash, and the scan opened the right Impressum. On Site 11 the scanner took the footer's "Datenschutzbestimmungen" link of the newsletter form, which opens the shop's privacy page as an overlay; the judge took both as right. The one accessibility link of the ten ("Digitale Barrierefreiheit", which the reviewer took for a statement) is "bitte manuell prüfen" in the final version, because the same text leads to a service page on another site (second sample).
- System: 10 of 10 right (also WooCommerce on 2 of 2 shops).
- Fields present on the ten Impressum pages: 33, all as text, none only as an image. Missed by the scanner although on the page: 2 of 33 (6 %), both Handelsregister: one written without "HRA"/"HRB" (only "Registernummer:" and digits under "Eintragung im Handelsregister"), one under the heading "Aufsichtsbehörde", which the scanner reads as a third party.

Second sample, after the fixes: because three of the fixes came from sites of the first sample, ten more sites were checked the same way with the fixed scanner, picked by the same kind of rule: every third site from Site 01 (Site 01, 04, 07, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22, 25, 28: Shopify 2, WooCommerce, Shopware, other, WordPress 3, Wix, Jimdo).
- Impressum fields reported as found: 33, all 33 the same as the reviewer. Wrong: 0. Fields present on the pages: 33, missed: 0.
- System: 10 of 10 right.
- Links: 1 wrong in that run. On Site 22, a web agency, the footer link "Digitale Barrierefreiheit" leads to the agency's service page, not to an accessibility statement; on Site 08 the same text and the same address lead to a real statement. A link alone cannot tell the two apart, so a link that names only the topic is now "bitte manuell prüfen", never "found" (`certain: false`); only texts or addresses that say statement ("Erklärung zur Barrierefreiheit", "Informationen zur Barrierefreiheit", "…-erklaerung") count as found. With that, links reported as found: 20 (10 Impressum, 10 Datenschutz), wrong: 0. One statement was missed: a PDF "Konformitätserklärung" behind a link with the text "Hier".

Code review: an independent review of the A4 code from three angles (templates; links, the second tab and timing; system detection) found 16 problems, and a second reviewer reproduced each of them on invented input. All 16 were fixed before the merge, each with a test. Links and system: an exact "Impressum" text now wins over a footer link that only contains the word (an article title in a footer widget), "contains" counts only for labels of up to four words, the address fallback needs a whole word; placeholder anchors ("#!", "#0", an empty href) are no links; for an anchor on the homepage (a one-page site) the fields are "bitte manuell prüfen" instead of searching the whole homepage text (which can hold addresses of client projects), and a link that leads back to the homepage is "failed"; links are compared with the document's own address; a homepage whose text could not be read reports no missing link; path signals of a system count only on the page's own host, and uploads and media files are weak; the Jimdo asset names match. Templates: a mailto or Cloudflare link counts only where the page shows it and not in someone else's block (a link without findable text only on the site's own domain); social and fediverse handles ("Instagram @…", "@user@host") and sentences with "at" are no e-mail addresses, and the top-level domain must exist (Public Suffix List; `.example` and `.test` for the fixtures); chamber and authority blocks (Ärztekammer, IHK, Kassenärztliche Vereinigung, …) are third parties; a year is no house number ("seit 1985"); a town ends at a phone label ("T.") and at the next word of a sentence, two-word towns need a prefix such as "Bad" or "Sankt"; the legal form "AG" is not read as Amtsgericht; a Gerichtsstand clause is no register court; the register entry of a general partner is skipped; thousands dots in register numbers are kept; the town pattern had exponential backtracking on repeated small words ("i. d." 30 times hung the analysis), and long single lines were quadratic: towns and courts are now read word by word and every window before a match is bounded (all hostile inputs of the review under 10 ms). The 30 saved captures were analysed again after the fixes, without visits: systems, links and fields unchanged, and 97 of 97 quotes of found fields are exact parts of the page text or link addresses.

Both samples together, final version: 64 Impressum fields reported as found, 0 wrong; 40 links reported as found, 0 wrong; system 20 of 20 right. Fields present on the 20 Impressum pages: 66, all as text; missed: 2 (3 %), both a Handelsregister entry.

What this means for stage D (Fedor's question about the Claude API): on these 20 sites the templates found 64 of 66 fields that were on the page; the two misses are a register entry without "HRA"/"HRB" and one under a heading that names an authority. No field was shown only as an image. Earlier in this session, before the fixes, the misses were 4 of 33 on the first sample. That is a low miss rate on this set, but 20 sites from one region are a small sample.
